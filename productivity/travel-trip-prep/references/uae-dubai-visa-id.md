# UAE / Dubai entry routes for an Indonesian passport

Verified from ICP (icp.gov.ae), GDRFA (gdrfad.gov.ae), the UAE Embassy Jakarta
and the Emirates / flydubai / Etihad visa pages. Fees and windows move — re-check
ICP + the airline page before quoting, and say so when you quote.

## 1. There is NO free visa for an Indonesian passport

The free 30-day and 90-day visa-on-arrival rosters are nationality lists
(Malaysia, Brunei, Singapore, Hong Kong, Kazakhstan, EU/US/UK/JP/KR/AU… —
Indonesia is absent). An Indonesian ordinary passport always needs a
pre-arranged visa, or a pre-approved entry permit.

## 2. The paid "Entry Visa for Nationals of Certain Countries" (ICP)

Indonesia was added to this scheme on 25 June 2026 (with Thailand, Vietnam, the
Philippines, Kenya, South Africa, alongside India).

- Categories: **14-day** (AED 100 issuance + AED 100 smart-services fee;
  extendable once for 14 more days, extension AED 250) and **60-day** (AED 250 +
  AED 100; single entry, **non-extendable**).
- 30-day pre-entry validity on the issued permit; service estimate ±2 days.
- Eligibility: ordinary passport valid ≥6 months **and** a valid residence permit
  (≥6 months validity) issued by the USA, an EU member state, the UK, Singapore,
  Japan, South Korea, Australia, New Zealand or Canada. Accompanying family
  members are checked individually.
- Applied for through ICP Smart Services (needs UAE Pass) / the airline's
  pre-approved channel — **not** handed out automatically at the airport.
- Overstay: AED 50/day.
- Reading trap: ICP and the UAE Embassy word it "visa on arrival", some agencies
  call it "pre-approved". Treat it as apply-before-flying and tell the traveller
  to confirm the current conditions with ICP or the airline before paying.

**Qualifying-country trap:** residence in a country that is not on the list
(Thailand's Non-B, a GCC residence, etc.) does not qualify. Only a listed
residence permit — or, per some agency readings of the US/EU/UK rows, a valid
visa — counts. If the traveller does not clearly qualify, send them down the
standard tourist route in §3 instead of guessing.

## 3. Standard pre-arranged tourist / visit visa (the default answer)

Apply through any of these, all online, no embassy visit for the e-visa:

- **Airline (simplest when the ticket is already booked)** — Emirates: apply in
  Manage Booking, itinerary must be all-Emirates in one PNR, ≥4 working days
  before arrival, VFS processing fee on top: tourist 30d single US$90, 30d multi
  US$136, 60d single US$177, 60d multi US$231; 48h transit US$10, 96h US$49.
  flydubai travel shops need a confirmed+paid round-trip with flydubai, minimum
  5 working days. Etihad only sells transit visas online.
- Hotel sponsorship, UAE travel agencies, or the UAE-resident sponsor route.
- VFS Global Jakarta (Kuningan City Mall Lt.2, for airline-sponsored apps).

Documents: passport (≥6 months, machine-readable, colour copy), colour photo
4.3 × 5.5 cm on a plain light background, confirmed hotel booking, return/onward
ticket, UAE-valid health insurance, plus relationship documents for dependents.
Never hand over a non-refundable ticket before the visa is approved.

## 4. Staying longer than a month

- Longest single entry first: 60-day tourist visa, then extend inside the UAE.
- In-country extension (GDRFA, 30 days): AED 600 + 10 + 10 + 5% VAT ≈ AED 630;
  ICP lists extend-entry AED 500 + AED 100 application. A tourism entry permit can
  be extended up to **120 days total**; friend/relative visits up to 180 days.
  A 30-day visa with two extensions reaches ~90 days, so plan the math before
  suggesting any exit/re-entry.
- **5-year multi-entry tourist visa** (GDRFA, self-sponsored): bank balance of
  US$4,000 (or equivalent) held over the six months before applying, 90 days per
  stay, extendable once for the same period, total ≤180 days per year, unused days
  do not carry over.
- Short layover: 48h / 96h transit visa (Etihad AED 55 / AED 216; Emirates the
  US$ figures above).
