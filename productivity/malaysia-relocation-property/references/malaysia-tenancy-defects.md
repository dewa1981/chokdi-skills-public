# Defects in a Malaysian rental — tenant rights & the escalation ladder

Use when the user living in (or renting out) a Malaysian condo/flat reports a defect:
inter-floor water leakage from the unit above, mould, a burst concealed pipe, water
coming through the ceiling. The law here is unusually pro-victim, and the timetable is
statutory — so the answer is a written, sequenced escalation, not "wait for the owner".

## Law and timetable (Strata Management Act 2013; Strata Management (Maintenance &
Management) Regulations 2015, regs 55–67)

1. **Complain in writing to BOTH the landlord/agent and the building management
   (JMB/MC)** — photos + video, the date and time the leak was first seen, and the damage
   caused. Written chat messages are usable evidence; verbal reporting is not.
2. **The management must issue a Certificate of Inspection (Form 28) within 7 days** to
   the complainant and the relevant owner, stating the cause and whether the parcel owner
   or the management is liable. Ask for it by name — staff move once they hear "Form 28".
3. **Costs follow the source.** A leak inside the upper parcel = that owner's liability;
   a failure of common property = the JMB/MC. Rectification includes **consequential
   damages** (belongings, ruined ceiling, mould remediation).
4. **If the source owner ignores Form 28 for 7 days, the JMB/MC has a statutory right to
   enter that unit, carry out the repair, and charge the cost back** to that owner's
   maintenance account. So the tenant is not dependent on the neighbour's goodwill.
5. **Escalate: Commissioner of Buildings (COB) → Strata Management Tribunal (SMT)** — the
   tribunal handles breaches of management duty, maintenance failures, charges and
   inter-parcel damage; it is not a forum for title disputes.

## What the tenant should ask for

- The repair **date and scope in writing**, plus the name of the appointed contractor.
- If the unit is not safely habitable (mould, water near electrics, wet children's beds):
  **temporary alternative accommodation or a rent adjustment**, and **keep every receipt**
  — those receipts become the consequential-damage claim.
- **Landlord's obligation:** structural and pre-existing plumbing is the landlord's
  responsibility; tenant-caused damage is the tenant's. Read the tenancy agreement first.
- Booked through **Airbnb / Booking**? Report on the platform immediately — relocation or
  refund usually moves faster there than with a private owner.

## Pitfalls

- **A rental defect is never a reason to overstay a visa.** Separate the two tracks out
  loud: the stay gets fixed legally (border run / fly home), and the landlord dispute can
  be pursued by chat from anywhere. See the trip skill for the stay mechanics.
- **Do not tell the tenant to "wait for the owner to come".** Waiting past the 7-day
  Form 28 window throws away the strongest legal lever they have.
- **Photograph and date everything from day one**, including damaged belongings, and note
  the cost of anything replaced or thrown out — without the dated record the claim shrinks
  to the repair cost alone.
- **Don't advise withholding rent unilaterally** — ask for a written reduction or an
  alternative unit instead; unilateral non-payment weakens the tenant's position.
