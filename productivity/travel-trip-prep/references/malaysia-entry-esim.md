# Malaysia (Penang) — entry + connectivity

## Entry
- Thais: visa exemption **30 days**, no visa needed. Count from the arrival stamp;
  give the user a last-safe-exit date with 1–2 days of buffer.
- **MDAC (Malaysia Digital Arrival Card)** — mandatory for every foreign traveller
  including infants. File at `imigresen-online.imi.gov.my/mdac/main` within
  **3 days before arrival** (travel day counts). Save a screenshot: immigration
  may ask, and the phone may have no data on landing.
- Overstay = per-day fine + blacklist risk. Staying longer: apply at **JIM
  (Jabatan Imigresen Malaysia)** for a short-term social visit pass (not
  guaranteed) or do a border run out of Malaysia and re-enter — mechanics, routes
  and the order to try them are in **"Staying longer"** at the end of this file.
- Keep proof of onward/return travel in the phone: airlines can refuse boarding
  on a visa-free one-way booking.
- Travelling with a child: passport plus birth certificate / house-registration
  copy, since the airline and immigration may ask to prove the relationship.

## Connectivity
- **Travel eSIM** (data-only, no phone number → calls over WhatsApp/LINE):
  Klook, Airalo (Maxis), Nomad, Saily, Travelsim Asia (Maxis + CelcomDigi).
  Rough pricing, verify at purchase: 5 GB/30d ≈ $8–12 · 10 GB ≈ $15–20 ·
  20 GB ≈ $21–32 · 50 GB ≈ $35–49.
- **Local prepaid** (passport required, buy at airport/7-Eleven) is usually the
  best value: CelcomDigi tourist 15 GB + unlimited calls/30d ≈ RM25, 65 GB ≈ RM39;
  Maxis/Hotlink and U Mobile "unlimited" are speed-capped 6–12 Mbps; Yes is
  cheapest but thinnest coverage.
- **Dual-SIM set-up**: data → travel eSIM; calls/SMS → home (Thai) line; Data
  Roaming ON for the eSIM; Low Data Mode on for the eSIM; app-store auto-update
  over cellular off.

## Data budget rules of thumb (10 GB / 30 days ≈ 340 MB per day)
- Maps + Grab + chat all day ≈ 20–80 MB/day → comfortable.
- TikTok/Reels 30 min ≈ 150–450 MB → half the daily quota.
- Kids' cartoons 1 h (480–720p) ≈ 300–700 MB → 9–21 GB/month on its own.
- Video call 30 min ≈ 150–300 MB → 4.5–9 GB/month if daily.
- Uploading one 1-minute video ≈ 60–150 MB → 2/day = 3.6–9 GB/month.
→ Content creator + toddler streaming = 20–30 GB/30d minimum; upload over Wi-Fi.

## Most accurate sizing method
Have the user read their own phone's data usage (iPhone: Settings > Cellular >
Current Period) for the last month and add ~30%. That beats every estimate table.

## Staying longer — extension vs border run (try them in this order)
1. **Extension at JIM is the fallback, not the first answer.** Per imi.gov.my,
   extending a short-term social visit pass is granted on **"special
   consideration"** (illness, accident, war at home) and requires evidence, a
   **confirmed return ticket**, form **IMM.55** and a personal appearance at a
   state office (Penang: Mon–Thu & Sat 08:00–17:00, Fri 08:00–17:15, closed
   Sunday), always **before** the pass expires. "We want to stay longer" and "our
   rental is being repaired" are not grounds — do not send her to queue first.
2. **A border run resets the 30 days**, but re-entry is a privilege, not a right.
   Expect questions from the 2nd–3rd run: carry onward ticket, accommodation
   booking, proof of funds. **Re-file the MDAC on every re-entry**, and photograph
   every entry stamp.
3. **Nearest Malaysian–Thai land crossings for a Penang-based traveller** (road
   distance/time from George Town, OSRM): **Bukit Kayu Hitam / Sadao (ด่านสะเดา)
   155–163 km ≈ 2 h 10 m — the closest** · Padang Besar (Perlis) 185 km ≈ 2 h 25 m ·
   Hat Yai 215 km ≈ 2 h 45 m (**Sadao → Hat Yai is 57 km ≈ 50 m**). Wang Prachan
   (Satun) sits far to the west — confirm the road distance before proposing it.
   Minivans Penang ↔ Hat Yai run 3 h 30 – 4 h 30 for ≈ RM70; the train via Padang
   Besar is the comfortable option with a toddler.
4. **Do the run 2–4 days before expiry, never on the last day**, and sleep one
   night on the Thai side instead of a same-day turnaround with a small child.
   Bring: all passports, the child's birth certificate / house-registration copy,
   the new MDAC and photos of the old stamps.
5. **If the destination is home anyway, just fly home.** Leaving Malaysia by any
   route closes the stay; re-entry is only needed if she intends to come back —
   quote the flight option next to the land route and let her pick.
6. **Verify geocoded border posts by the returned state/country.** A plain
   "Wang Prachan" query resolved to Suphan Buri (1,244 km away) on the first hit;
   always add province + country and sanity-check the coordinates against the
   expected highway corridor before quoting a distance.
