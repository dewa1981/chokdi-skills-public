# Posting schedule & cadence for a Thai-audience content page

## When to Use

- "เวลาโพสคอนเทนต์ควรเป็นช่วงเวลาไหน", "how often should I post", "when should I go live".
- Scheduling or planning a week/month of clips for a Facebook page, TikTok, Reels or Shopee Video.
- Posting from abroad (timezone convert) or while low on time (batch-schedule during the child's nap).
- Cross-posting a clip between platforms, or pinning affiliate links.

Class of task: "เวลาโพสคอนเทนต์ควรเป็นช่วงเวลาไหน", "how often should I post", "when should I go
live", and the follow-on work of scheduling clips for a personal content/affiliate page
(mum-and-child niche, Thai audience). Answer with the audience's clock, not the platform press kit.

## Thai-audience windows (verified 2026 — re-check yearly, they drift)

- **TikTok — the clear peak is 18:00–22:00.** Secondary: **06:00–08:00** (waking, receptive —
  educate/inspire) and **11:00–13:00** (lunch scroll — short, light, funny).
- **TikTok day-of-week:** Tue–Thu are steadiest; Fri 16:00–19:00 rises; **Sunday ~20:00 is the
  weekly peak**. In Thailand Saturday morning and evening still perform (the US "Saturday dead
  zone" does not transfer).
- **Facebook page:** 07:00–12:00 on weekdays, Thursday having the longest peak; weekend afternoons
  are the weak slot. A second push at 20:00–21:00 is acceptable.
- **Instagram:** 10:00–15:00, with an 18:00 bump.
- **Live shopping** (TikTok Shop / Shopee live): **19:00–22:00** is the conversion window; product
  review/beauty clips ride 18:00–21:00.
- **Niche beats generic:** for a mum audience the true windows are **when the child sleeps** (after
  bedtime) and the quiet mid-day nap hour. Ask when the audience is actually alone with the phone
  before quoting any table.
- **Never recycle US/EU "best time to post" tables** — collected in other timezones, several of
  their golden hours land at 02:00–04:00 Thai.

## Timezone trap while travelling

Peninsular **Malaysia is UTC+8 — one hour ahead of Thailand (UTC+7)**. Schedule in Thai time and
convert to the phone's local clock (**+1 hour**), or set the scheduler's timezone to Asia/Bangkok.
Getting this wrong silently moves the post off the peak.

## Tools so the schedule does not cost sleep

- **Meta Business Suite** (free) schedules Facebook + Instagram ahead; a **TikTok business
  account** schedules ahead too. For a parent who only gets the phone when the toddler naps, plan
  and load the week during the nap instead of being awake at the target hour.

## Cadence and algorithm mechanics

- **Consistency > exact minute.** One short clip a day beats bursts.
- TikTok **batch-tests a new clip against a few hundred viewers in roughly the first 30 minutes** —
  be available to answer comments in the first hour, or schedule it for a slot you are free in.
- Space multiple posts **3–4 hours** apart; do not publish three clips inside one hour.
- Pin the **affiliate link in the first comment**, not in the caption.

## Test-then-lock method (instead of arguing about tables)

Run **two weeks × three slots** — 07:00 / 12:30 / 20:00 Thai — with the same style of clip, then
read the page's own data: Facebook **Professional Dashboard → when your followers are online**,
TikTok **Analytics → Followers → activity**. Lock the two winning slots as the standing schedule.
Say plainly that the page's own numbers outrank any published table.

## Affiliate / cross-posting rules that break reach or pay

- **TikTok audio is licensed for TikTok only.** Reposting a downloaded TikTok clip to Shopee Video
  or Reels carries the watermark and the copyrighted track — export a clean master (no logo) and
  swap the music.
- Commission logic: **only completed orders earn**, and it lands after the order completes
  (~15 days); friends/family repeatedly buying through one's own link can flag the affiliate
  account. Commission is unaffected by the customer's country.
- **Shopee/Lazada apps are country-locked** — a Thai account cannot switch to a Malaysian store;
  cross-border shopping needs its own account and payment path. Say so before recommending a store.

## Deliverable preference for this user

Hand over a **4-week posting table as a real file** (`.xlsx`/`.docx`) with the slots pre-filled and
columns for views/comments — not a chat wall of prose. Keep chat to Thai bullets, money and times
labelled.

## Pitfalls

- Do not answer with a bare list of times: pair each window with what to **post** in it
  (educate at dawn, entertain at lunch, sell at night).
- Do not confuse the **creator's** local time with the **audience's** time when the creator is abroad.
- Do not recommend times from memory of another niche — ask what the audience does at that hour
  (asleep, commuting, feeding a child).
- Do not schedule a clip into a window the user cannot answer comments in, then blame the algorithm.
