# Staying in Malaysia: entry, passes, and schooling

## Entry

- **MDAC** mandatory for all travellers including infants, submitted within **3 days before arrival**, free, only on the official immigration portal (`imigresen-online.imi.gov.my/mdac/main`). Screenshot the confirmation.
- Thai passport: **30 days visa-free**. Days count from the **entry stamp**, so read the stamp, not the ticket.
- No onward ticket on a visa-free entry = airline can refuse boarding; a cheap outward booking solves it.

## Student Pass (school level)

- Available to non-citizens **aged 3 and above**, pre-school through secondary.
- Applies to government/government-aided schools, international schools, expatriate schools, private schools and Chinese private secondary schools that carry MOHA authorisation for international students.
- Application may be submitted by a legally appointed school representative, a parent, or a legal guardian.
- Student Pass fee RM60; social visit pass for dependants/guardians of a Student Pass holder RM90.

## Guardian / dependant of a Student Pass holder

- Permitted guardians: biological parents, legal guardians, adoptive parents, grandparents, siblings under 7 years old.
- **Guardians may not work, do business, or lecture politically** — social-visit basis only.
- Typical documents (new applications): IMM.12, school request/appointment letter, passport copies (>=18 months validity), photo, Malaysian or international health insurance >=12 months, stamped personal bond, 3 months' bank statements, birth certificate (translated) and, if relying on a spouse's income, marriage certificate. Renewals use IMM.55 + IMM.38.
- Guardian Pass practice: one parent, issued ~12 months and renewed annually, **refundable bond RM10,000** for many nationalities, proof of funds required, renewal contingent on the child's Student Pass staying valid and attendance >=80%.
- Practical consequence: the child must be enrolled and holding a Student Pass **first**; the parent's pass follows. A child under 3 has no Student Pass, hence no guardian route.

## Other long-stay bases

- **MM2H** — tiers (Silver/Gold/Platinum) with fixed-deposit and property requirements; pass holders can sponsor a Dependent Pass.
- **Employment Pass** — from 1 Jun 2026 EP categories I, II and III can all sponsor dependants. Spouse and children under 18 = Dependent Pass (a child on a DP may enrol in school without a separate Student Pass); parents, adult children and unmarried partners = LTSVP.
- **Postgraduate Student Pass** can sponsor dependants.
- Relatives other than spouse/children/parents give no route.

## Schooling near Bandar Seri Alam (JB "City of Knowledge")

- **Taska / childcare** ~0–4 years; **tadika / kindergarten** ~3–6; international early years from ~3–4. Fees: tadika roughly RM300–600/month, international school RM25,000–57,000/year.
- Kindergartens in Bandar Seri Alam: **Tadika Matahari – Seri Alam** (Jalan Tasek 52, minutes from the Jalan Tasek housing corridor); **Little Caliphs Kindergarten Bandar Seri Alam** (Islamic).
- International/private schools: **Japanese International School** (Persiaran Seri Alam); **Repton International School (Seri Alam)** — formerly Excelsior, British curriculum, 20-acre campus; **Invictus International School (Masai)** — opened 2024, English National Curriculum with Cambridge assessments, Foundation Stage 1 about RM25,500/yr rising to about RM56,500 at Year 13; **UniWorld International School** (business precinct); **Foon Yew High School – Seri Alam** (Chinese-medium).
- School naming changes fast (rebrands, group ownership). Tell the user to confirm the current operating name, curriculum and fee schedule directly with the school before planning around it.

## Housing anchors near Bandar Seri Alam

- Midas @ Seri Alam (Persiaran Seri Alam): completed 2023, 35 floors, ~1,609 units, freehold, non-Bumi, facilities incl. pool/gym/playground/minimart. Sells ~RM230k–440k (2-bed 667 sq ft about RM330k; 3-bed 840 sq ft about RM388k–440k); rents about RM1,400–1,800/month.
- Other strata in the township: Citrine Residenz, Anjung Seri, Opal Residenz (new phase); Bandar Alam Masai is the neighbouring township.
- Amenities within the township: Lotus's Seri Alam, Hospital Pasir Gudang (government), KPJ Pasir Gudang Specialist (private), SMK Seri Alam.
