# FB page mechanics (Patty's page เรื่องราวของลูกรักน้องสโนว์)

## @ tagging followers — truth
- Tagging does NOT reliably push posts to followers; tags notify but algorithm decides reach.
- Limit ~50 tags/post technically, but >10–15 looks spammy → post demoted, people block page.
- Only tag people who follow/friend the page. Tag 2–5 relevant engaged people max.

## What actually grows reach (legit)
1. Ask fans to set **เห็นก่อน (See First)** — safest, posts always on top.
2. Share into relevant Thai mom/local groups (แม่ๆ, กลุ่มเชียงราย).
3. Reply to comments in first hour; end captions with questions.
4. Consistent short Reels + Auto Captions.
5. Ask for shares politely in caption.

## Professional Dashboard "ภารกิจ/achievements รายสัปดาห์"
- The weekly progress/achievements (e.g. 10% done, 6 days left) = optional gamification.
- NO penalty for skipping; completing gives small visibility boost. Don't stress Patty about them.
- Meta Verified badge on dashboard may be a PAID subscription — if she's not using it, check billing and cancel.

## Golden posting windows (from prior advice)
- เช้า 6–8, เที่ยง, เย็น 18–21 น. — timezone ICT.

## C2C Non-CB affiliate lesson (commissions)
- Order type "C2C (Non-CB)" = consumer-to-consumer, no cashback → NEVER commission-eligible regardless of who cancelled.
- Commission final only after delivery + receipt + no return (~15 days).
- Friends/family buying through her link repeatedly = detection → commission cut + account ban. Advise: ห้ามชวนเพื่อน/ครอบครัวซื้อช่วย.
