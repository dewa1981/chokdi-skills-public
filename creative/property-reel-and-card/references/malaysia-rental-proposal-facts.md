# Malaysia rental proposal — verified facts & reusable copy

Depth for property proposals (Klang Valley condo/serviced residence). Re-verify anything numeric before
putting it in front of a client; treat the bands below as "last seen", not permanent.

## Connectivity facts worth citing (verified against the operator)

- **Sunway Nexis, Kota Damansara (Seksyen 5, Petaling Jaya)**: an **80 m covered pedestrian bridge** links
  **Entrance B of Surian MRT Station to Level 1** of the development. Opened **13 May 2017**; MRT Corp billed
  it as the first link bridge in Malaysia connecting an MRT station to a private development.
- **Surian** is on the **Kajang Line** (MRT Sungai Buloh–Kajang): direct ride toward TRX / Bukit Bintang /
  Maluri, with interchanges onward. The building car park also serves as a park-and-ride for commuters.
- Source of record: the MRT Corp (`mymrt.com.my`) event page for the Surian–Sunway Nexis link bridge.
- General rule: state bridge length, which entrance, opening year and the line — those four are checkable.
  Do not quote "2 minutes' walk" from an ad as if it were measured.

## Rent benchmarks (asking, last checked Nov 2026)

- Sunway Nexis 1+1 / 1-bedroom, ~870–900 sq ft, furnished: asking **RM2,800–3,500 per month**.
- A quote of **RM3,800** therefore sits above the band — a proposal must justify it (larger built-up,
  higher floor, better furnishing, two parking bays) rather than assert it is market rate.
- Asking ≠ achieved. Never present listing rent as the transacted rent for the building.

## Deposit norms

- **2 + 1** (two months security + one month utility/advance) is the market norm.
- **3 + 1** is common for fully furnished units.
- A **full month's rent as a utility deposit** is above norm — utility deposits are usually RM500–1,000.
  Put it in the document as a negotiation point; hiding it makes the proposal look padded.

## Reusable "why rent here" bullets (adapt, don't paste verbatim)

- *No car needed* — covered bridge to the platform; no traffic, toll or parking, rain or shine.
- *Real monthly saving* — transit-linked living usually removes one car: roughly RM600–800/month in
  instalment, petrol, toll and parking.
- *Move in with a suitcase* — fully furnished, so no furniture outlay (easily RM15,000–30,000 from empty).
- *Everything downstairs* — podium retail (F&B, grocers, clinics, services); nearby malls a short walk away.
- *Secure and practical* — 24-hour security; car park doubles as park-and-ride.
- *Mature township* — schools, medical centres, malls, deep F&B; not a construction-site neighbourhood.

## Pre-signing checklist to carry in every proposal

Built-up area and bedroom count · floor level and view · number of car park bays · tenancy term and
renewal option · who pays maintenance and sinking fund · utility account transfer · minor-repair clause ·
conditions for deposit refund.

## Copy deck for the money block

| Line | Figure |
|---|---|
| Monthly rental (fully furnished) | quoted rent, reference only |
| Security deposit — N months | rent × N |
| Utility deposit — N month(s) | rent × N |
| First month rental | rent |
| **Total due at signing** | deposits + first month |

State in one line beneath the table that the recurring rent row is for reference and is not added again —
otherwise a reader who sums every row gets a larger number than the stated total.
